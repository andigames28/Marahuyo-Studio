ABOUT US

Marahuyo Studio is a creative photoshoot studio celebrating personal style, self-expression, and Filipino-inspired fashion. From gyaru looks to Filipiniana-inspired concepts, we create memorable photoshoot experiences where everyone can feel confident, creative, and uniquely themselves.

★ ★

OUR ADVOCACY

Our photography highlights and promotes the mix of Filipino culture with Japanese culture: Gyaru. Our studio aims to capture and show both countries’ traditional style while being able to keep and complement each of their uniqueness.
